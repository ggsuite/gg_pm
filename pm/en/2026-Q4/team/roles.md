# Roles

## Developer

- Responsible for: building and testing the tickets
- People: none yet
- Hears about: finished tickets, changed requirements, decisions, schedule shifts of their tickets

## Product Owner

- Responsible for: goals, requirements and their priority
- People: none yet
- Hears about: goals, requirements, decisions, schedule shifts

## Management

- Responsible for: budget, staffing and the target dates
- People: none yet
- Hears about: goals, reached and missed target dates, risks to the schedule

## Sales

- Responsible for: what is offered to customers and when
- People: none yet
- Hears about: finished features and their dates; no internals

## Customer

- Responsible for: using the product and giving feedback
- People: none yet
- Hears about: finished features and their dates; no internals
